Pre-Launch Operational Checklist

A step-by-step master checklist of actions required across business settings, inventory, payments, courier logistics, and deployment before opening Gao Essentialls to public customers.

STEP 01

Store & Business Information (Admin Settings)

  • Real Banking Details for EFT Payments Action Required
    In Admin → Settings, input your verified South African business bank account (Bank Name, Account Holder, Account Number, Branch Code). These details appear on customer invoices and order confirmation screens.
  • Official WhatsApp & Telephone Numbers Action Required
    Enter your primary WhatsApp Business contact number. Customers use this line to send EFT payment proofs and ask pre-purchase questions.
  • Customer Care Email Address
    Set an active company email (e.g. orders@gaoessentialls.co.za) to receive customer messages from the Contact page and checkout confirmation copies.
  • Warehouse / Store Collection Address
    Provide the exact street address in Johannesburg where customers choosing "Store Collection" will pick up their orders, along with collection hours (e.g. Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00).
  • Announcement Banner Customization
    Update the top notification banner (e.g. "Free nationwide delivery on orders over R1,000 | Cash on Delivery available").
STEP 02

Product Catalogue & Inventory Finalization

  • Audit Mock Seeded Products Action Required
    Review the 41 existing sample products across Clothes, Shoes, Appliances, Pots, Bags, Blankets, Hats, and Jerseys. Delete items you don't stock or edit them to reflect actual inventory.
  • Accurate Selling & Promotional Prices
    Confirm the correct regular retail price and optional sale/discount price in South African Rand (ZAR) for each product.
  • Initial Physical Stock Counts
    Enter true unit counts for all items. Products with 0 stock automatically lock and show "Sold Out", while items below 5 units display an urgent "Only X left" badge.
  • Size & Colour Options Setup
    Verify that variant selectors match your physical stock (e.g., S/M/L/XL for clothes & soccer jerseys; UK 4–11 for shoes; litre capacity like 5L/7.5L for pots and air fryers).
  • Upload Real Product Photography
    Use the Admin photo uploader to add real multi-angle photographs for any new incoming stock (JPEG, PNG, or WEBP up to 5MB).

STEP 03

Payment Methods & Financial Workflows

  • Cash on Delivery (COD) Rules & Validation Ready
    COD is 100% active on the store. Confirm which metropolitan zones you service for cash handling and establish an SMS/WhatsApp verification step before dispatching COD packages.
  • EFT Proof of Payment Verification Protocol
    Establish a daily routine to check your business bank account against customer order numbers (e.g. GAO-10045) before changing an order's status to "Confirmed" in Admin.
  • Online Card Payment Gateway Integration Optional
    If you wish to accept Visa/Mastercard cards and Instant EFT, create a merchant account with PayFast, Yoco, or Ozow and complete FICA verification.
STEP 04

Courier Logistics & Packaging Materials

  • Courier Partner Account Setup Action Required
    Open a commercial business account with a nationwide courier such as The Courier Guy, Pudo, Fastway, or Aramex.
  • Delivery Fees & Free Shipping Threshold
    Set your flat delivery rate (e.g. R99 or R120) and your Free Shipping threshold (e.g. R1,000) in Admin → Settings to match your courier contract rates.
  • Packaging Supplies & Waybills
    Ensure you have flyer bags, sturdy cartons, bubble wrap (critical for pots and air fryers), and label printing paper ready at your dispatch station.
STEP 05

Legal Compliance & South African Consumer Laws

  • Return & Refund Policy (CPA Compliant) Recommended
    Define your return window (standard 7–14 days for unused goods in original packaging), warranty on electric appliances, and who covers return courier fees.
  • Privacy Policy (POPIA Compliant)
    State that customer names, physical addresses, and contact numbers are stored securely and used solely for fulfilling orders and courier delivery.
STEP 06

Security, Domain & 24/7 Cloud Deployment

  • Change Default Admin Password Action Required
    The current default password is Admin@123. Change this immediately to a secure 12+ character private password.
  • Register Official Domain Name
    Acquire gaoessentialls.co.za (or .com) through Domains.co.za, Namecheap, or 1-grid.
  • Deploy to 24/7 Cloud Server
    Deploy to a cost-effective cloud VPS (DigitalOcean/Hetzner ~$5/month) or Vercel so the store runs around the clock with free SSL (HTTPS) encryption.
  • Live Dry-Run Test Order
    Place an actual order on your smartphone, check the receipt, confirm it appears in the Admin dashboard, and verify the order tracker updates cleanly.
